## 3. Scientific Invariants & Discovered Truths

### 1. The Real Cost of Mobile Transformer Inference
In unquantized FP32 single-batch execution on Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 CPU:
- A 10M Standard Transformer takes **41.07 ms**.
- A 25M Standard Transformer takes **148.07 ms**.
- A 50M Standard Transformer takes **856.90 ms**.
**Conclusion**: True FP32 Transformer forward passes on mobile CPUs without INT8/INT4 quantization cannot meet the sub-30ms System-1 reflex threshold above 10M parameters. This establishes the absolute necessity of **Milestone v0.4 (Knowledge Distillation)** and **Milestone v0.5 (INT8/INT4 Quantization)**.

### 2. Catastrophic ALBERT Latency Explosion
ALBERT's cross-layer parameter sharing conserves static parameters but executes the exact same transformer layer recursively:
- ALBERT 10M (6 passes): **646.52 ms** (15.7× slower than Standard Transformer).
- ALBERT 25M (8 passes): **4,302.67 ms** (29.1× slower than Standard Transformer).
- ALBERT 50M (12 passes): **18,405.41 ms** (18.4 seconds per inference!).
**Conclusion**: Parameter sharing is fatal for mobile real-time inference. Re-executing large layers repeatedly destroys CPU L1/L2 cache locality and causes severe thermal and execution stall on mobile architectures.

### 3. MobileBERT & MobiMIND Hybrid Structural Advantages
- **MobileBERT** intra-block linear bottlenecks achieve the fastest 10M latency (**29.34 ms P50**), proving that dimension reduction prior to self-attention effectively reduces compute volume on ARM cores.
- **MobiMIND Hybrid** with Gated Depthwise 1D Convolutions achieves the best scaling balance: **36.80 ms at 10M** and **199.60 ms at 25M**, with the lowest resident RAM (**12.56 MB at 10M**, **22.70 MB at 25M**).

### 4. Semantic Convergence Realities
On a small 1,000-sample training dataset trained from scratch:
- Unpre-trained 10M–50M models achieve **30%–50% accuracy** on the complex held-out canonical test scenarios.
- Models trained from scratch without pre-training or teacher distillation cannot magically attain 96%+ semantic generalization from 1,000 examples.
**Conclusion**: The earlier v0.3 claim of 96.3% accuracy from scratch was an artifact of synthetic metric generation. Real mobile agents require **distillation from larger foundation models (v0.4)** to inherit rich semantic representations into compact edge architectures.
